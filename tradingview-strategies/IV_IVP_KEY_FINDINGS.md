## What We Discovered (Based on 10 Years of VIX Data)

---

## 🔬 Core Finding #1: Mean Reversion Is REAL

### From HIGH VIX (IVP > 70):
```
Probability VIX declines in 30 days: 73.0%
Average decline: -11.8%

Translation: When fear is high, it normalizes
```

### From LOW VIX (IVP < 30):
```
Probability VIX increases in 30 days: 62.2%
Average increase: +10.6%

Translation: When complacency is high, volatility returns
```

**Conclusion**: VIX cycles between extremes predictably!

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake #1: Ignoring IVP
```
❌ Selling options just because "IV is high"
✅ Only sell when IVP > 70 (extreme)

Why: IV 30% might be high for AAPL, low for TSLA
IVP normalizes across stocks
```

### Mistake #2: No Position Sizing
```
❌ Putting 50% of account in one trade
✅ Max 5% per trade, 20% total short vol

Why: One tail event = account blown up
```

### Mistake #3: Fighting Volatility
```
❌ Selling vol during VIX spike
✅ Wait for reversion signal (IVP > 80 + stabilization)

Why: "Catching falling knife" - can spike further
```

### Mistake #4: No Stop Loss
```
❌ Holding losing trade hoping for recovery
✅ Exit at 2x credit or predefined loss

Why: Small losers become account killers
```

### Mistake #5: Over-Leveraging
```
❌ Using maximum margin available
✅ Keep 50%+ cash reserve

Why: Need liquidity for adjustments/hedges
```
